## What to build

Connect the existing tab/window closure workflow to the recording owner's protected close operation. Accepting closure immediately protects its pending log work, Continue waiting keeps that protection, and the same close remains able to accept a later explicit loss decision. The log-loss choice reflects recording work rather than unrelated transport cleanup.

Related existing work: [#30](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/30), [#34](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/34), [#35](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/35), [#36](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/36), [#37](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/37). These references identify established behavior or adjacent acceptance work; they are not blocking dependencies and are not to be reopened or reimplemented by this ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Acceptance of explicit close joins or starts one recording close and protects its admitted work before waiting for an existing pause/EOF/disconnect drain, final cutoff, the five-second question or the user's answer. A prior completed I/O failure is not reversed.
- [ ] Only unresolved recording work triggers the existing five-second log-loss choice. Transport-only cleanup does not trigger it; Continue waiting preserves the existing timer/choice without repeated prompts and prevents automatic ten-second abandonment.
- [ ] Keep a later explicit close-with-unsaved-data action accessible on the same close owner. Forward that decision through the complete recording operation without a caller cleanup recipe or a new allowance; when required cleanup admission is unavailable, report it and preserve the pending wait.
- [ ] Loss permission abandons only unissued work and requests cancellation of issued work where possible. Preserve issued buffers, file handles and charges until actual completion, including blocked synchronous flush; cleanup adoption and visual removal do not prove actual release.
- [ ] Distinguish save success, incomplete/uncertain recording, pending release and cleanup adoption. A late loss answer cannot damage a completed save or another generation, and late completion cannot overwrite an established incomplete outcome with an unqualified save-success claim.
- [ ] Repeated tab/window/bulk/automatic closure requests join existing recording work and decisions through the already specified closure owner. A missing view, cancelled observation or failed question presentation supplies no implicit loss permission and creates no cyclic completion wait.
- [ ] Use localized English/Japanese presentation for any affected visible choice/status. Verify through real WPF closure and session/Rust recording paths with controlled blocked writes/flushes, clocks, actual I/O errors, late completion and cleanup-capacity pressure across both five- and ten-second thresholds.
- [ ] Verify recording-versus-transport completion using the existing transport/session seam, including a completed log with transport cleanup still held. The close owner depends on the recording result and does not require the new transport interface to distinguish those outcomes.
