## Parent

[Deepen recording, transport, persistence, vault, SSH and grapheme ownership](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/40)

## What to build

Deepen the existing C# recording owner so the actual session and recording controls request complete pause, resume and explicit retry operations. The owner retains file completion, failure and release duties after a caller stops waiting, while preserving the already specified Rust recording content and interval behavior. This implements the whole-codebase Q1-Q3 ownership refinement to the existing native-recording work rather than rebuilding its projections.

Related existing work: [#27](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/27), [#28](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/28), [#29](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/29), [#30](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/30). These references identify established behavior or adjacent acceptance work; they are not blocking dependencies and are not to be reopened or reimplemented by this ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Route production pause, resume, retry and initial recording preparation through named complete operations on one session-generation recording owner; ordinary callers no longer assemble native cutoff, writer completion, durable flush and handle-release steps or own operation leases.
- [ ] Expose bounded immutable results/status and a read-only actual-release observation. Distinguish inactive/no-change, accepted work, successful save/pause, failed or incomplete recording, and resources still owned; cancelling observation does not cancel the accepted operation.
- [ ] Preserve whole-selected-format preparation before transport startup, disabled recording behavior, interval eligibility, fresh parts without backfill, and cancellation before activation. A partial open never activates a subset; its original owner closes successful handles and leaves created files as already specified.
- [ ] A successful Pause requires the ordered Rust cutoff, completion of accepted writes, durable flush and part close. Actual write/flush/close errors stop group admission without replay, guessed byte counts or damage to healthy terminal effects and replies.
- [ ] Keep the 8 MiB group allowance, approximately one-second batching and ten-second pending-I/O no-progress policy. Idle time without pending I/O does not fail recording; issued and failed-but-owned buffers remain charged until actual completion.
- [ ] Explicit Retry creates fresh part owners and numbers. Delayed opening or prior-part completion/failure cannot activate or mutate the replacement; unavailable existing cleanup capacity rejects or defers new preparation visibly without accumulating owners.
- [ ] Exercise the ordinary production interface with real Rust semantics, independent expected log bytes, controlled blocked/partial/failed/late file I/O and time, plus real-file and recording-control integration. Preserve the native content cases from the related issues rather than replacing them with helper-count assertions.
