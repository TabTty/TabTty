## What to build

Route receive EOF and disconnect through the same complete recording owner used for pause and retry. A peer ending its receive direction flushes the eligible received content once while preserving recording-part ownership and any permitted opposite-direction interaction; a later pause, disconnect or close shares that work instead of repeating it.

Related existing work: [#27](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/27), [#29](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/29), [#30](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/30), [#37](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/37). These references identify established behavior or adjacent acceptance work; they are not blocking dependencies and are not to be reopened or reimplemented by this ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Add and use named complete receive-completion and disconnect-completion operations on the existing session-generation recording owner, preserving the distinction from Pause and final Close.
- [ ] Apply source decoder/parser finalization and eligible Text tails once at their ordered receive cutoff; flush accepted work through that position while EOF retains part/session ownership and does not globally pause otherwise permitted local-echo recording. SSH local echo remains disabled.
- [ ] Preserve reverse input where the transport permits it after receive EOF. Retained disconnected output remains usable, and EOF or recording completion never claims to release transport or retained-terminal ownership.
- [ ] Repeated EOF, disconnect after EOF, pause after EOF and overlapping drains share admitted I/O and once-only terminal finalization while reporting each operation's own completion condition.
- [ ] Complete only the required session ordering step from EOF/disconnect callbacks. Do not await aggregate close/session completion that depends on that callback, and retain observation and original-generation cleanup when a caller stops waiting.
- [ ] Verify exact Raw/Text/Terminal bytes and file lifetime through real Rust/session paths for split source tails, EOF then permitted input, EOF followed by pause/disconnect, repeated facts, overlapping pause drains, failed flush and delayed old-generation completion.
