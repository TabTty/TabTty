## Parent

[Deepen recording, transport, persistence, vault, SSH and grapheme ownership](https://github.com/TabTty/TabTty/issues/40)

## What to build

Connect the SSH terminal path to the complete terminal-payload transport seam and the shared SSH channel owner first exercised by SFTP. Early DATA/stderr is processed before PTY/shell readiness, protocol control progresses during a slow terminal consumer or blocked send, and startup/closure results preserve the original session's output and resource ownership.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Bind the typed terminal consumer and current committed geometry before transport I/O; eliminate the SSH transport's dependency on constructing or calling a concrete terminal-session class. Reuse the shared channel owner for terminal open/PTY/shell/send/credit/close instead of copying its state, request or ownership machinery.
- [ ] Preserve the existing host-trust, authentication, crypto and secret-lifetime owners. Deliver early DATA/stderr in source order while PTY/shell startup is pending, including output preceding refusal, timeout or cancellation; readiness follows protocol success and consumption of prior facts and never activates a stopped generation.
- [ ] Use current dimensions when requesting the PTY after authentication/dialog delays. Coalesce subsequent startup geometry to the latest permitted notification without occupying the session reducer, and prevent stale geometry results from replacing current commitments.
- [ ] Return eligible receive credit only after Rust processing, recording admission or its narrow failure, bounded reply admission and allocation release. Do not wait for generated-reply transmission, disk flush or painting; keep window adjustments, rekey and closure progressing with zero send credit or a held consumer.
- [ ] Account advertised credit, outstanding reads, packed payload, source metadata and control work together. Verify more than 1,024 tiny alternating DATA/stderr messages and metadata-only pressure without unbounded item growth, silent loss or a healthy-looking deadlock; use recoverable pressure or the existing affected-session failure scope.
- [ ] Use shared send outcomes to expose definitely submitted complete input units promptly for echo/pacing, preserve partial/uncertain suffixes without replay, and distinguish Stop Paste, abandoned observation and Disconnect. Preserve the existing unit boundaries supplied by the input owner, including the amended scalar/codec-safe Stop when that input slice is integrated.
- [ ] Treat exit-status, exit-signal and other terminal-channel metadata as ordered inert facts, never terminal/log payload or implied EOF; valid later output and permitted reverse traffic continue. While application consumption is paused, mandatory replies including unknown want-reply requests remain bounded, ordered and once-only, and make protocol progress independently of the terminal callback.
- [ ] Keep directional EOF and final cutoff separate. During the existing up-to-two-second peer-close grace, continue valid terminal delivery until channel/protocol/transport termination; do not treat late post-termination socket bytes as payload. EOF/closed facts hand off recording completion without awaiting it or claiming recording/retained-output release.
- [ ] Startup failure and cancellation settle previously admitted delivery against its original generation. Consumer failure after partial mutation stops ordinary delivery without replay, false credit or dependence on a final closed callback; independent status reports the failure and actual resources stay owned through delayed I/O.
- [ ] Verify the ordinary session/Rust/log path with controlled SSH packet/consumer/time/I/O schedules and real scripted-peer coverage for early output followed by failure, startup resize, zero-credit replies, rekey, partial sends, EOF with permitted reverse input and late generation cleanup. Exercise concurrent SFTP traffic to prove payload isolation and shared-writer progress, while retaining separate SFTP parsing/reopen acceptance.
- [ ] Remove the obsolete session-facing raw read/acknowledge and concrete-session startup paths for SSH after its consumers migrate; preserve independent protocol vectors and the dedicated SFTP API. No external SSH client library or new terminal implementation is introduced.
