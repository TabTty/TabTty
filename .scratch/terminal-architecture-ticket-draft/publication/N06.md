## What to build

Keep the authoritative terminal in Rust when the user resizes after reception. Reflow normal output and parked normal content while alternate content clips or pads according to the existing terminal contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reflow only soft-wrapped normal logical lines and preserve hard boundaries, attributes, whole clusters, content anchors, revisions, and recording provenance.
- [ ] Clip/pad alternate content without reflow and reflow parked normal content once at the final dimensions when returning to it.
- [ ] Enforce captured physical-row retention before publication. Preserve the distinction between quota retirement and explicit erase/delete/overwrite; expose eligible retirement effects without inventing LF.
- [ ] Admit peak resize state and owned results before mutation, reject without a partial geometry change, and retain accurate ownership of snapshots held across resize and disposal.
- [ ] Verify continuous resize, wide/combining text, retained-row boundaries, alternate transitions, selection/copy, and disconnected retained tabs through the real session and presentation paths without managed handoff.
