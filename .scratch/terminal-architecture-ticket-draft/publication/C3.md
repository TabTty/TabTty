## What to build

Route window-close intent through a complete operation that joins already accepted tab closures and obtains the applicable combined confirmation for remaining targets. Close the window when its actual targets satisfy the existing removal requirements, without requiring a second user request.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] When tab A is already closing and waiting for cleanup, closing its A/B window joins A, reviews B and other newly affected targets as required, and completes without another close click.
- [ ] Declining the later review leaves newly affected targets untouched while A continues. Outcomes distinguish joined or independently completed effects from effects introduced by the later request.
- [ ] Joining repeats none of A's confirmation, termination, recording finalization, transfer-window closure, release request, cleanup charge, timer, or loss decision. A prior Continue waiting choice remains authoritative.
- [ ] Overlapping reviews revalidate target ownership, committed executions, placement, and approval scope. Invalid approval ends the affected request with a changed-target explanation and no automatic reconfirmation loop.
- [ ] Requests wait on target executions rather than mutually dependent window requests. Disconnect, final-tab, and native window-close callbacks observe or revalidate existing execution without cyclic waits or duplicate disposal; coordinator ordering is not held across dialogs or asynchronous cleanup.
- [ ] A failed or pending required target removal prevents false whole-window success. Final visual commit includes all tab kinds and preserves existing settings/editor behavior, movement guards, activation ordering, and final-exit policy.
- [ ] Real WPF window-close interactions and session/resource integration cover A/B joining, refusal, overlapping reviews, duplicate native callbacks, target failure, settings/editor tabs, activation and movement races, and final-window exit through visible outcomes and actual ownership.
