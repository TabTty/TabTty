## What to build

Serve rendering, copy, UIA, and existing AI screen/history capture from the authoritative Rust terminal through bounded owned immutable results. Preserve stable content and retention evidence without constructing a mutable managed terminal replica.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Route the actual presentation and observation callers to native captures, preserving normal, parked normal, alternate, retained disconnected, and reconnect-generation behavior without managed handoff.
- [ ] Preserve distinct cell, Unicode-scalar, grapheme, UTF-16, and UTF-8 units at the seam, with stable content anchors through pure reflow and accurate collapse or loss behavior after eviction.
- [ ] Maintain bounded intrinsic revision/retention metadata for subsequent validation, detecting intervening edits even when final text is restored. Attribute/layout-only changes retain their distinct semantics.
- [ ] Reserve requested result ownership before copying. UIA full-range requests either return the complete contractually requested result or explicitly fail, without implicit truncation or invented pagination.
- [ ] Keep snapshots and observation results valid across later mutation and terminal disposal, account held results until release, and preserve existing capture lifetimes, layout epochs, and input-completion watermarks.
- [ ] Verify real render/copy/UIA/history paths with reflow, eviction, alternate screens, disconnect, and disposal. Ordered receive observers must not steal data or require rendering; add no macro runtime or speculative macro API.
