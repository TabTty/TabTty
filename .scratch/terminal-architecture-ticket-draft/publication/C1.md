## What to build

Make terminal-initiated transfer-window closure observe the existing file-operation owner, apply its cleanup and reporting policy, and return the actual outcome. This preparatory vertical slice makes the existing visible close paths safe to reuse without introducing a new transfer policy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Repeated transfer-window close requests observe the same execution and result; an already-closing window is not reported as completed cleanup.
- [ ] Known unresolved temporary paths and unknown outcomes are reported before dismissal where required. Failure to present a required report prevents silent dismissal and produces a bounded failure result.
- [ ] Issued provider work and its resource charges remain with the actual owner until completion. Visual dismissal, permitted cleanup adoption, and actual completion remain distinct.
- [ ] Existing terminal-tab and terminal-window close paths consume the transfer outcome and do not claim successful closure when required dismissal or reporting failed.
- [ ] Retained disconnected transfer windows preserve their results. Tab movement preserves their association with the terminal tab and connection generation.
- [ ] Real WPF close interactions and file-operation/provider integration verify normal completion, delayed cleanup, duplicate requests, unresolved outcomes, and failed reporting through visible results and actual ownership.
