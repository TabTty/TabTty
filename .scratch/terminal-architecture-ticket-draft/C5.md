# Retain native terminal state until final tab removal

## What to build

Connect the complete closure operation to the Rust terminal owner's final lifetime. Disconnect preserves authoritative native state for retained output; committed final tab removal releases that ownership deterministically while independent results and issued I/O retain their own lifetimes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Retained disconnected output remains available from Rust. Receive EOF and transport cleanup do not destroy the retained terminal state or select a managed terminal fallback.
- [ ] Successful final visual removal releases the applicable terminal ownership exactly once through its owner. A failed removal preserves usable state for the surviving view.
- [ ] Tab movement, pane removal, and WPF unloading do not dispose the terminal session. Old-generation completions cannot release or mutate a replacement session.
- [ ] Held immutable snapshots and results remain valid and charged until their independent release. Issued I/O and its buffers retain their original owner and charges until actual completion.
- [ ] Real native/session recording integration verifies once-only receive and EOF finalization, eligible tail delivery, and independent transport and log cleanup. Visual removal and release do not imply a successful durable save.
- [ ] Production-interface tests plus actual WPF tab-close and retained-output interactions verify held results, duplicate requests, failed removal, movement, delayed I/O, and deterministic release without relying on garbage collection.
- [ ] The implemented ownership integration is exercised through the real single-tab close and shared per-target execution. Other closure commands reuse that release boundary when their own slices are integrated; complete native-only cross-command acceptance belongs to the final integration gate.

## Blocked by

- T11 — Own complete native recording interval operations.
- T18 — Close a terminal tab through one owned operation.
